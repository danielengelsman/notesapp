# Bookstead public-claims fact-check (adversarial)

Date: 2026-07-11. Method: every factual claim on the five public pages + the video voiceover script was independently re-verified against the live web (search + fetch; several vendor pages 403 direct fetch — those verdicts rest on multiple independent search excerpts, noted inline). Company research files were consulted only to compare what the site *relied on* vs what it *published*. Code-level claims (security page) were verified against the actual repo.

**Bottom line: the headline price is stale.** Intuit raised Pro Plus again effective **Feb 1, 2026: $999 → $1,149/yr** (Kemper CPA, Brady Martz, Insightful Accountant — all three independently). The site's own research (research-gtm.md line 10, research-pricing.md §"Pro Plus") already said this, but the published pages went with the *October 2025* number ($1,049). Every "$1,049 in 2026" instance — including two spoken lines in the recorded videos — is wrong today.

## The single worst finding

**The company's flagship number is wrong in its own favor's opposite direction — and it's recorded in the videos.** Every instance of "Pro Plus renewal 2026: $1,049/yr" (hero badge, pricing table, and spoken in voiceover lines L1 and F3) is stale: Intuit's Feb 1, 2026 increase took renewals to **$1,149/yr**, and the company's *own research files* (research-gtm.md, research-pricing.md) say exactly that — the site copy just ignored them. For a product whose entire trust argument is "every market claim has a source," shipping a headline price that its own published sources contradict is the one error a hostile commenter will find in 30 seconds.

Runner-up (biggest product risk rather than credibility risk): the lapsed-subscriber flow claims view-only mode "can still run reports **and exports**." Report → Excel export is confirmed by Intuit; the **File → Utilities → Export → Lists to IIF** step that Bookstead's own Step 1A requires is confirmed nowhere, and the company's research explicitly flagged it "must be product-tested." If IIF export is blocked in view-only mode, the advertised rescue path for the most motivated customers doesn't work as documented.
