## 1. QBD installed base and decline trajectory

- Intuit does NOT disclose a QuickBooks Desktop subscriber/user count in its recent 10-K filings (checked FY2021–FY2025 filings via search; only revenue is broken out). [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687825000035/intu-20250731.htm] [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687822000028/intu-20220731.htm]
- QuickBooks Desktop Accounting revenue was $1,672M in fiscal 2025 (Intuit FY ends July 31), with Desktop Services and Supplies at $1,103M — Desktop Ecosystem total ≈ $2.78B. Seen in search summary of the FY2025 10-K; exact figures UNVERIFIED (filing itself not fetchable through proxy). [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687825000035/intu-20250731.htm]
- Desktop Ecosystem revenue increased $134M, or 5%, in fiscal 2025 vs fiscal 2024, "due to higher effective prices and changes made to QuickBooks desktop offerings beginning in early fiscal 2024 to complete the transition to a recurring subscription model." [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/896878/000089687825000035/intu-20250731.htm]
- Fiscal 2024: Desktop Ecosystem revenue grew 16% for the year and 25% in Q4 FY2024, attributed to concluding a three-year transition to a subscription model. [https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1202/intuit-reports-strong-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-results-sets-fiscal-2025-guidance-with-double-digit-revenue-and-earnings-growth]
- Q1 FY2025: Desktop Ecosystem revenue DECREASED 17% (subscription-transition comparison effect). [https://investors.intuit.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1223/intuit-reports-strong-first-quarter-results-and-reiterates-full-year-guidance]
- Q3 FY2026 (quarter ended Apr 30, 2026): Desktop Ecosystem revenue increased $42M, or 6% — i.e., desktop revenue is still GROWING as of mid-2026, on price, not units. [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000896878/000089687826000025/intu-20260430.htm]
- Third-party estimate: "over one million active Desktop users" as of 2026, with Intuit still shipping updates/fixes/support. Vendor-blog estimate, not an Intuit disclosure — treat as order-of-magnitude. [https://www.fourlane.com/blog/state-of-quickbooks-desktop-for-2026/]
- For scale context: QuickBooks Online had ~6.5M subscribers as of 2023, roughly 65% of a ~10M QuickBooks customer base. [https://fitsmallbusiness.com/quickbooks-statistics/] [https://www.acecloudhosting.com/blog/quickbooks-intuit-stats/]
- 84.15% of QuickBooks Desktop customers are in the United States (6sense install-tracking estimate). [https://6sense.com/tech/small-business-accounting/quickbooks-desktop-market-share]
- QuickBooks overall holds ~81% US small-business accounting software market share (vs Sage 50 ~10%, Xero ~9%) per stats aggregators. [https://electroiq.com/stats/quickbooks-statistics/] [https://www.acecloudhosting.com/blog/quickbooks-market-share/]
- Implied paying-subscriber math (MY COMPUTATION, not a source): $1,672M FY2025 Desktop Accounting revenue ÷ blended ARPU of ~$1,000–$1,600 (Pro Plus $1,049 single-user … Enterprise $1,703–$5,200+) ⇒ roughly 1.0–1.6M paying QBD subscriptions worldwide, ~0.9–1.4M US (84%). Consistent with Fourlane's "over one million active" figure. Legacy perpetual-license holders (QBD 2021 and earlier, now all service-discontinued) are additional but uncounted anywhere — UNVERIFIED, plausibly hundreds of thousands.

## 2. QBD subscription price history 2021–2026 (documenting the hikes)

- Pro Plus:
  - 2021–2022: $349.99/yr (1 user). [https://www.merchantmaverick.com/complete-guide-to-quickbooks-desktop-pricing/]
  - 2023 release: $549/yr. [https://www.hayniecpas.com/quickbooks-2023/]
  - Effective Oct 1, 2024: $999/yr for existing subscribers. (Intermediate steps between $549 and $999, if any, UNVERIFIED.) [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/account-management/quickbooks-desktop-plus-subscription/00/1499653]

- Enterprise (annual, 1 user, 2026): Silver ~$1,703, Gold ~$2,210, Platinum ~$2,717, Diamond custom (~$5,200+, monthly billing). [https://verito.com/blog/quickbooks-enterprise-pricing/] [https://www.acecloudhosting.com/blog/quickbooks-enterprise-pricing/]

[https://www.kempercpa.com/news/upcoming-changes-to-quickbooks-desktop-pricing-effective-february-2026] [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/intuit-subscriptions/understand-changes-per-employee-multi-company-file/L4uifTLCZ_US_en_US] [https://www.gotomyerp.com/quickbooks-desktop-2026-pricing-changes/]

## 3. Lifecycle dates: stop-sell, discontinuations, and what actually stops working

### Stop-sell (new subscriptions)
- Intuit announced it would stop selling QuickBooks Desktop Pro Plus, Premier Plus, Mac Plus, and Enhanced Payroll to NEW US subscribers after July 31, 2024; the deadline was later extended to September 30, 2024. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/whats-new/quickbooks-desktop-stop-sell/] [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/new-subscriptions/us-quickbooks-desktop-sold-july-2024/L5lkQNq7L_US_en_US] [https://outoftheboxtechnology.com/blog/intuit-extends-deadline-new-quickbooks-desktop-subscriptions]
- Existing subscribers CAN keep renewing after Sept 30, 2024, and continue receiving security updates, product updates, and support. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/new-subscriptions/us-quickbooks-desktop-sold-july-2024/L5lkQNq7L_US_en_US]
- QuickBooks Desktop ENTERPRISE is NOT affected — still sold to new customers in 2026 with no announced end date. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/r/whats-new/quickbooks-desktop-stop-sell/] [https://www.method.me/blog/quickbooks-desktop-discontinued/]
- US-only change. [https://uhy-us.com/insights/news/2024/march/quickbooks-desktop-products-no-longer-available-to-new-us-subscribers-after-july-31]
- QuickBooks Desktop 2024 is the last annual version for Pro/Premier (no 2025/2026 releases); Intuit now ships periodic "R" updates (R9, R10, R11…) to version 24.0 instead of annual versions. [https://www.bizbooks.pro/blog/quickbooks-desktop-discontinued.html] [https://www.sdocpa.com/quickbooks-desktop-discontinued/]

### Service discontinuation schedule (each May 31)
- QBD 2021: all services discontinued May 31, 2024. [https://www.rklesolutions.com/blog/quickbooks-desktop-retiring-may-2025]
- QBD 2022 (Pro/Premier/Mac/Enterprise v22): discontinued May 31, 2025. [https://commercetech.com/blog/your-quickbooks-desktop-2022-software-will-be-discontinued-after-may-31-2025/] [https://www.rklesolutions.com/blog/quickbooks-desktop-retiring-may-2025]
- QBD 2023: discontinued May 31, 2026 — i.e., JUST died six weeks before today (mid-2026). This is the freshest urgency wave. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/feature-preferences/quickbooks-desktop-service-discontinuation-policy/L17cXxlie_US_en_US] [https://www.certumsolutions.com/library/quickbooks-desktop-2023-service-ending-may-2026]
- QBD 2024: CONFLICTING. Standard policy would imply May 31, 2027; multiple 2026 sources instead say support runs through September 30, 2027; at least one source claims v24.0 "won't be discontinued in May 2027" and gets continuous support indefinitely. UNVERIFIED — the exact next-wave date is uncertain. [https://clonepartner.com/blog/quickbooks-desktop-discontinued-full-sunset-timeline-through-2027] [https://wizcommerce.com/blog/how-long-will-quickbooks-desktop-be-supported-discontinuation/] [https://www.sdocpa.com/quickbooks-desktop-discontinued/]

### What stops at SERVICE DISCONTINUATION (version sunset)
Per Intuit's discontinuation policy and coverage of it: after the May 31 cutoff, that version loses live support and all connected services — Desktop Payroll (no tax-table updates, no forms, no data send), Desktop Payments (no card/check processing), online bank feeds (no transaction downloads, online payments, transfers), emailing forms/reports from within QB — and receives no further security updates. Critically, the software still OPENS: users can open the company file, enter transactions manually, and run reports; Intuit does not lock the file on June 1. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/feature-preferences/quickbooks-desktop-service-discontinuation-policy/L17cXxlie_US_en_US] [https://www.certumsolutions.com/library/quickbooks-desktop-2023-service-ending-may-2026] [https://cloudtopoffice.com/will-quickbooks-desktop-stop-working-2026/]

### What stops at SUBSCRIPTION NON-RENEWAL (Plus subscriptions — different and harsher)
- When a Pro Plus/Premier Plus subscription lapses, QuickBooks switches to VIEW-ONLY mode for ONE YEAR: the user can open the company file and view data but cannot add transactions, run reports, or make edits. After that year, view-only access ends too. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/cancel-products-services/renew-subscription-use-view-mode-quickbooks-2023/L4IUEYTMg_US_en_US]
- Intuit states users still "own their data" regardless of subscription status and can open/edit files with a compatible license or active subscription — but for post-2022 subscription-only versions there is no perpetual license to fall back on. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/account-management/what-happens-when-my-qb-desktop-subscription-lapses/00/1351250] [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/account-management/what-happens-when-your-subscription-expires/00/940021]
- THIS is the strongest factual foundation for Ledgerlift's archive product: a 2022+ Plus subscriber who stops paying $1,049+/yr gets 12 months of look-but-don't-report access, then nothing. (Whether view-only mode permits data EXPORT is not established by these sources — must be product-tested.)

## 5. IRS record-retention rules (the archive need)

Per IRS guidance for small businesses (irs.gov):
- General rule: keep records 3 years from the date the return was filed (period of limitations). [https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records]
- 6 years if income was underreported by more than 25% of gross income shown on the return. [https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records]
- 7 years for claims of loss from worthless securities or bad-debt deduction. [https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records]
- Employment tax records: at least 4 years after the tax becomes due or is paid, whichever is later. [https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/employment-tax-recordkeeping]
- Indefinitely if no return was filed or a fraudulent return was filed. [https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records]
- Practical upshot: a business that stops paying for QBD in 2026 must still be able to produce 2020–2026 books through at least 2030 (and payroll detail through ~2031) — but Intuit's view-only window is only 12 months. This gap is the product's legal-need engine. (Property/basis records must be kept even longer — until the limitations period expires for the year the property is disposed of. [https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/how-long-should-i-keep-records])

2. Stranded segment = Pro Plus / Premier Plus / Mac Plus subscribers (stop-sold Sept 2024, single remaining version 24.0, renewal $1,049–$1,469+/yr) — assume 60–75% of the unit base (Enterprise skews revenue, not units): ~550K–1,000K US businesses. UNVERIFIED split; Intuit does not disclose it.
3. Exit events: with renewals up ~3x since 2021 and QBD 2023's May 31, 2026 sunset just past, assume 15–30% of the stranded base exits desktop per year, 2026–2029 ⇒ ~450K–800K cumulative US "leave QBD" events, plus perpetual-license holders finally forced off dead versions.
4. Every exit event needs (a) a data landing zone and (b) 3–7 years of retrievable history (IRS rules above). Intuit's free path covers (a) only if they buy QBO ($456–$3,300/yr) and their file is under 750K targets.
5. Gross TAM for Ledgerlift Pro at $149 ($99 launch): 450K–800K events × $99–149 ≈ $45M–$120M through ~2029. Bookkeeper tier: ProAdvisor population size UNVERIFIED (Intuit publishes no current count) — treat the $499/10-file tier as upside, not base case.
6. Realistic SAM (owners who self-serve a browser tool + bookkeepers doing decommissions): plausibly 10–20% of gross TAM ⇒ $5M–$25M. A 2–5% capture of SAM ⇒ roughly $250K–$1.25M revenue — a solid indie/small-team business, not venture-scale. All step-3/6 percentages are ASSUMPTIONS, not sourced facts.

## RED FLAGS

1. **Desktop isn't dying on schedule.** Enterprise is still sold to new customers with no announced end date, QBD 2024 (v24.0) receives continuous "R" updates, and existing Plus subscribers can renew indefinitely. Intuit's Desktop Ecosystem revenue GREW 5% in FY2025 and 6% in Q3 FY2026. The "evacuate now or lose everything" urgency applies to specific cohorts (lapsing Plus subscribers; dead-version holdouts), not the whole base. [https://www.method.me/blog/quickbooks-desktop-discontinued/] [https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000896878/000089687826000025/intu-20260430.htm]
2. **Discontinuation does NOT lock files.** After a version sunset the software still opens, accepts manual entries, and runs reports locally forever. Perpetual-license holders (2021 and earlier) therefore have a free do-nothing archive option, weakening the archive pitch for exactly the oldest cohort. The hard lockout only hits post-2022 subscription lapsers (view-only 1 year, then nothing). [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/feature-preferences/quickbooks-desktop-service-discontinuation-policy/L17cXxlie_US_en_US] [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/cancel-products-services/renew-subscription-use-view-mode-quickbooks-2023/L4IUEYTMg_US_en_US]
3. **Export-access dependency (product risk flagged by market facts).** If a target user has ALREADY lapsed into view-only mode, sources say they cannot run reports — it is unestablished whether they can still export data for Ledgerlift to parse. If not, the addressable moment narrows to "before lapse," which changes all marketing timing. Must be product-tested. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/learn-support/en-us/help-article/cancel-products-services/renew-subscription-use-view-mode-quickbooks-2023/L4IUEYTMg_US_en_US]
4. **No authoritative installed-base number exists.** "Over one million active users" is a reseller-blog estimate; Intuit discloses only revenue. TAM could be materially smaller (or larger, counting legacy perpetual users). Every TAM figure above inherits this uncertainty. [https://www.fourlane.com/blog/state-of-quickbooks-desktop-for-2026/]
5. **QBD 2024 sunset date is unresolved** (May 31, 2027 vs Sept 30, 2027 vs "continuous support, no sunset"). The size and timing of the next urgency wave — the business's main 2027 marketing beat — cannot yet be stated with confidence. [https://clonepartner.com/blog/quickbooks-desktop-discontinued-full-sunset-timeline-through-2027] [https://wizcommerce.com/blog/how-long-will-quickbooks-desktop-be-supported-discontinuation/]
6. **Intuit gives migration away free** (Dataswitcher, batch tools, in-product move; possibly "1.5M migrated" already — UNVERIFIED), and QBO's first year is heavily discounted. Ledgerlift's wedge must be the archive + reconciliation proof + non-Intuit destinations, not migration per se. [https://quickbooks.intuit.com/online/move-to-online/switch-to-quickbooks-online/]
7. **Verification debt.** Because the egress proxy blocked direct page fetches, key numbers (FY2025 revenue split $1,672M/$1,103M; QBO Aug-2026 price claims; "3 million Plus subscribers"; ProAdvisor counts) were seen only in search-result summaries. Re-verify against primary pages before publishing any of them in customer-facing material.
